Agency FAQ

Questions
agencies ask.

Can an agency nominate on behalf of a client?

Yes. Enter your own details as the nominator and your client's contact as the nominee. Your client then gets a short nominee form to complete.

Can we nominate more than one client?

Yes. Each nomination is separate, so you can enter as many clients, leaders or programs as fit the categories. Entering several? Email hello@causecatalystawards.com about agency pricing.

Who is eligible?

Nonprofits, human services programs and their leaders across the United States and Canada. Self-nominations are welcome too.

What does a nominee get right away?

As soon as the nomination is complete, the nominee receives an Official 2027 Nominee badge for their website, newsletter and social posts, and a Cause Catalyst nominee card in the mail.

When are winners announced?

Winners are announced November 11, 2027.
