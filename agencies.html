For Agencies & PR Firms

Award opportunities for your nonprofit clients.

Cause Catalyst is a national award for nonprofits, human services programs and their leaders across the US and Canada. Agencies can nominate clients directly, and every nominee gets recognition they can share the day their nomination is complete.

Why it works for your clients

  • National recognition focused on the work human services organizations actually do.
  • An Official 2027 Nominee badge and a nominee card in the mail as soon as the nomination is complete, not months later.
  • Nominations are scored on a standardized rubric: outcomes, client-centered service and continuous improvement.
  • Winners receive a trophy and a public announcement they can use with funders, boards and local press.

How to nominate a client

  1. Pick the category that fits your client best.
  2. Submit the nomination form with your details as the nominator and your client as the nominee. It takes about 15 minutes.
  3. Your client completes a short nominee form and can add references.
  4. The nominee badge goes out as soon as their nomination is complete.

Questions agencies ask

Can an agency nominate on behalf of a client?
Yes. Enter your own details as the nominator and your client's contact as the nominee. Your client then gets a short nominee form to complete.
Can we nominate more than one client?
Yes. Each nomination is separate, so you can enter as many clients, leaders or programs as fit the categories.
Who is eligible?
Nonprofits, human services programs and their leaders across the United States and Canada. Self-nominations are welcome too.
What does a nominee get right away?
As soon as the nomination is complete, the nominee receives an Official 2027 Nominee badge for their website, newsletter and social posts, and a Cause Catalyst nominee card in the mail.
When are winners announced?
Winners are announced November 11, 2027.
